    How to verify a NorAI credential

    1. Fetch norai.json and /.well-known/jwks.json over HTTPS from norai.si.
    2. Remove the signatures field, canonicalise the rest with JCS (RFC 8785) and verify the JWS EdDSA (Ed25519) signature with the key whose kid the header names.
    3. Check that the kid is the RFC 7638 thumbprint of the key.
    4. Check validity.expires and that the kid is not listed in revocations.json.
    5. Verify the operator’s declaration: declaration.txt.asc must be signed by the OpenPGP key whose fingerprint appears in norai.json and whose ID the operator publishes in the DNS record _aid-sysop.norai.si (a channel independent of the website). Its technical annex gives the SHA-256 of manifest.json, which lists the SHA-256 of every published file, and the hash of the previous declaration.
    6. To know you are talking to NorAI right now, and not to someone who copied these files, require a liveness proof: a signed HTTP request (Web Bot Auth) or a challenge signed with the identity key.
